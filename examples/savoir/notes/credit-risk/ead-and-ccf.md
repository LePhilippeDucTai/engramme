# EAD and credit conversion factors

The exposure at default (EAD) of a credit line includes the part of the
undrawn commitment that the obligor is expected to draw before defaulting.

:::cloze {#c-w2hy8m}
For a revolving credit line, $\mathrm{EAD} = \text{drawn} + {{c1::\mathrm{CCF}}} \times {{c2::\text{undrawn}}}$.
:::

:::qa {#c-5nz0ub}
Why do obligors draw more on their credit lines as they approach default?
---
They run short of cash and use the liquidity still available to them, starting with their committed lines.
:::
