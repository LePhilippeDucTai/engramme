# Expected loss

The one-year expected loss of an exposure is the product of its three risk
parameters:

$$
\mathrm{EL} = \mathrm{PD} \times \mathrm{LGD} \times \mathrm{EAD}
$$

Provisions and pricing cover the expected loss; capital covers the unexpected
loss.

:::cloze {#c-d7c4ge}
$\mathrm{EL} = {{c1::\mathrm{PD}}} \times {{c2::\mathrm{LGD}}} \times {{c3::\mathrm{EAD}}}$
:::

:::qa {#c-m1q8vk3}
What is the one-year expected loss of an exposure of 1,000,000 with a PD of 2% and an LGD of 40%?
---
$0.02 \times 0.40 \times 1\,000\,000 = 8\,000$.
:::

:::qa
Does IRB capital cover the expected or the unexpected loss?
---
The unexpected loss; provisions and pricing cover the expected loss.
:::
