# Probability of default

The probability of default (PD) used for regulatory capital is the probability
that an obligor defaults within one year, estimated as a long-run average of
one-year default rates.

:::qa {#c-4f7k2q}
Over what horizon is a regulatory PD estimated?
---
One year.
:::

:::cloze {#c-9xm3ta}
An obligor is in default when it is more than {{c1::90 days}} past due on a material credit obligation, or when it is {{c2::unlikely to pay::qualitative criterion}}.
:::

:::qa {#c-t8b2wz}
What is the difference between a point-in-time and a through-the-cycle PD?
---
A point-in-time PD moves with current economic conditions; a through-the-cycle PD is a long-run average over a full economic cycle.
:::
